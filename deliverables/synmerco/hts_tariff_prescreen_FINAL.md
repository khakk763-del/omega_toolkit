### REASONING

Heading 7323 covers table, kitchen or other household articles of stainless steel only in general terms; a vacuum vessel is specifically provided for in heading 9617, which is the more specific description, so 9617.00.1000 beats the stainless-steel household-articles line 7323.93.0080.
